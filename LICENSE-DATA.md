# Data licences

The code is MIT (`LICENSE`). The data comes from two government sources and keeps their terms.

- **CBSA** (Canada-bound wait estimates and the monthly service-standard history):
  Contains information licensed under the
  [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  No CBSA names, crests or logos are used, and nothing here suggests official status or endorsement.
- **CBP** (US-bound wait estimates): public domain. Source: U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

The values are wait **estimates published by CBSA and CBP**, not measured crossing times.
